Wefty's Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Wefty keeps your notes so you can use them on all your devices. No ads, no selling your data, no trackers. Wefty's team never reads your notes.

What Wefty keeps

  • Your e-mail address and name, and your password (scrambled: nobody can read it).
  • If you sign in with Google: the name and e-mail address Google gives us.
  • What you put in Wefty: notes, folders, flash cards, tests, reminders, pictures, files, recordings and your settings.
  • Which devices you use Wefty on (like "Android phone" or "Chromebook"), the Wefty version, and when you last used it.
  • That you agreed to the Terms, and when they were dated.

Each of your devices also keeps its own copy, so Wefty opens fast and works offline. On a PC with Wefty's app, the PC keeps your sign-in and that copy, and files you save from Wefty go into a folder in your Downloads.

Where it's kept

In the Wefty cloud, which runs on Supabase (a cloud database company), in a data centre in Virginia, in the United States. And on your own devices.

Who can see it

  • You. Nobody else using Wefty can see your notes.
  • Whoever runs the cloud can technically see what's stored in it: that's how any cloud works. Wefty's team never reads your notes. We only look at counts for Wefty's stats: how many people, notes and files, how much space, which devices and versions, and when people last used Wefty. Never what's in your notes.
  • Locked notes are scrambled on your device with your own PIN before they're saved, so nobody can read them without it, not even whoever runs the cloud.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No selling or renting your data, to anyone.
  • No trackers or analytics on Wefty's pages.

E-mails

Wefty only e-mails you about your account: a code to confirm your e-mail, to sign in, or to pick a new password. No newsletters or marketing.

AI is optional

AI only works with your own key, from Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude). What you send to it goes straight from your device to them, under their terms and privacy policies. Google's free Gemini may use what you send to improve its products, and people at Google may read it, so don't send anything private to it. Google requires the person making a Gemini key to be 18 or older, so a parent or guardian can make it with you. Your key is kept in your account's private storage, which other people using Wefty can't reach.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings > Account > Export everything gives you a copy of all your notes and files.
  • Delete: Settings > Account > Delete my account removes your account and all your notes and files from the cloud. The cloud's own backups keep a copy for a few days at most, then it's gone. The copies on your devices go when you sign out or remove Wefty there.
  • You can ask us anything about your data, or ask us to delete it, at wefty.info@gmail.com.

Children

We recommend that people using Wefty are 13 or older. Wefty doesn't ask anyone's age or birth date. If we're told that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it and everything in it.

Where Wefty is offered

In Canada and the United States.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll change the date at the top, and tell you in Wefty about anything big.

Questions? Write to wefty.info@gmail.com.
