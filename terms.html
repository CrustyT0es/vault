Wefty's Terms

Last updated 8 November 2026

Wefty is a free notes app for school, in beta. Be kind, keep it legal, and keep a copy of anything important. Your notes are yours.

What Wefty is

Wefty is a free app for notes, flash cards, tests and reminders, on a phone, a Chromebook or a computer. It's in beta: it's new, it's still being built, and it may change, break or stop. We work hard to keep your notes safe, but we can't promise Wefty will always work or always be there.

Who can use Wefty

  • Wefty is offered in Canada and the United States.
  • We recommend that you're 13 or older. Wefty doesn't ask your age.
  • If we're told that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it and everything in it. A parent, a guardian or the child can ask us at wefty.info@gmail.com.

Your account

  • One account per person. Use your own e-mail address.
  • Keep your password to yourself. Only type a code from another device when that device is right next to you, never one someone sent you.
  • You're responsible for what's done with your account.

Your notes are yours

You own what you write and add to Wefty: notes, pictures, files and recordings. You let Wefty store them and show them to you on your devices, and send them where you ask (to your other devices, or to an AI when you ask it something). Nothing more.

What's not allowed

  • Anything illegal.
  • Bullying, threatening or harassing anyone, or sharing someone's private details to hurt them.
  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18.
  • Viruses or anything else harmful, or attacking Wefty, its cloud or anyone's account.
  • Trying to get into someone else's account or notes.
  • Using bots or scripts to make accounts or send e-mails, or getting round Wefty's limits.
  • Selling or renting out Wefty, or pretending to be Wefty.

When we may remove an account

If an account breaks these rules, we may remove it with its notes and files. When we can, we'll e-mail you first to say why.

AI is optional

Wefty's AI features only work with your own key, from Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude). What you send goes straight from your device to them, under their terms and privacy policies, not ours. Google requires the person making a Gemini key to be 18 or older, so a parent or guardian can make it with you. Everything else in Wefty works without AI. AI can be wrong: check what it writes.

Free, with no ads

Wefty is free. There are no ads, and we never sell your data.

Storage limits

Each account has a limit on how much it can store, so the cloud stays healthy for everyone. Wefty shows yours in Settings.

No promises

Wefty is given as it is, without any promise that it works without mistakes or that your notes will never be lost. Keep a copy of anything important: Settings > Account > Export everything. As far as the law allows, Wefty isn't responsible for lost notes or for anything else that happens because of using it.

Leaving

You can stop using Wefty at any time. Settings > Account > Delete my account removes your account and all your notes and files.

Changes to these terms

We may change these terms. We'll change the date at the top, and tell you in Wefty about anything big.

Questions? Write to wefty.info@gmail.com.
